ANALYSIS 01 · PHYSICAL OCEANOGRAPHY

Sea Surface Temperature Variability in the Mozambique Channel

Research Question

How does sea surface temperature vary spatially and seasonally across the Mozambique Channel?

Objective

The objective of this analysis is to characterize the spatial and temporal variability of sea surface temperature (SST) in the Mozambique Channel using real oceanographic data.

Dataset

Dataset: NOAA Optimum Interpolation Sea Surface Temperature (OISST) v2.1

Variable: Sea Surface Temperature (SST)

Spatial resolution: 0.25° × 0.25°

Temporal resolution: Daily

Study period: 2020–2025

Study region: 10°S–25°S and 35°E–48°E

Methodology

The dataset will be spatially subsetted to the Mozambique Channel and processed to obtain monthly and seasonal statistics.

The analysis will include spatial averages, temporal variability, seasonal climatology and sea surface temperature anomalies.

Planned Results

  • Mean spatial distribution of SST
  • Monthly SST variability
  • Seasonal SST climatology
  • SST anomaly patterns
Results pending data processing.

Numerical results and scientific interpretations will be added after the real NOAA OISST dataset has been downloaded, processed and analysed.

No numerical result has been fabricated for this preliminary version of the analysis.
