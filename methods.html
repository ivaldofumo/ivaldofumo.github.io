Data & Methods

Study Region

The analyses focus on the Mozambique Channel, approximately between 10°S–25°S and 35°E–48°E.

The same geographical domain is used for the sea surface temperature and chlorophyll-a analyses, allowing physical and biological variables to be examined within the same oceanographic region.

Data Sources

Sea Surface Temperature

NOAA Optimum Interpolation Sea Surface Temperature (OISST) v2.1.

Variable: Sea Surface Temperature (SST)

Spatial resolution: 0.25° × 0.25°

Temporal resolution: Daily

Chlorophyll-a

NASA Ocean Color products.

Variable: Chlorophyll-a concentration (CHL)

Unit: mg m⁻³
