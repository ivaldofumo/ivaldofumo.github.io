ANALYSIS 02 · BIOLOGICAL OCEANOGRAPHY

Chlorophyll-a Variability in the Mozambique Channel

Research Question

How does chlorophyll-a concentration vary spatially and seasonally across the Mozambique Channel?

Objective

The objective of this analysis is to characterize the spatial and temporal variability of chlorophyll-a concentration and identify patterns associated with ocean productivity.

Dataset

Dataset: NASA Ocean Color

Variable: Chlorophyll-a concentration (CHL)

Unit: mg m⁻³

Study period: 2020–2025

Study region: 10°S–25°S and 35°E–48°E

Methodology

Chlorophyll-a data will be spatially subsetted to the Mozambique Channel and processed to examine spatial distribution, temporal variability and seasonal patterns.

The analysis will also distinguish coastal and open-ocean patterns where appropriate and will investigate the relationship between chlorophyll-a concentration and sea surface temperature.

Planned Results

  • Mean spatial distribution of chlorophyll-a
  • Monthly chlorophyll-a variability
  • Seasonal chlorophyll-a patterns
  • Coastal versus open-ocean patterns
  • Relationship between SST and chlorophyll-a
Results pending data processing.

Numerical results, maps, graphs and scientific interpretations will be added after the real NASA Ocean Color dataset has been downloaded, processed and analysed.

No numerical result has been fabricated for this preliminary version of the analysis.
