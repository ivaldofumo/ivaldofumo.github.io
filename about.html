ABOUT THIS PROJECT

About the author

Ivaldo de Jesus Fumo

Oceanography graduate and MSc student in Applied Oceanography at Eduardo Mondlane University, Mozambique.

My academic interests include physical oceanography, ocean–climate interactions, remote sensing, oceanographic data analysis and the application of Earth observation data to understand marine and coastal environments.

This digital portfolio was created as part of my Applied Oceanography studies and provides a space for documenting analyses based on real oceanographic datasets.

Purpose of this portfolio

The main purpose of this website is to develop practical skills in oceanographic data processing, visualization, statistical analysis and scientific interpretation.

The analyses presented here use real datasets from recognized scientific data providers and are designed to connect quantitative results with oceanographic processes.

Main areas of interest

  • Physical Oceanography
  • Ocean–Climate Interactions
  • Remote Sensing
  • Oceanographic Data Analysis
  • Earth Observation
  • Marine and Coastal Environments
