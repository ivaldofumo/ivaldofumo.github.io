About this project

This digital space was developed as part of my studies in Applied Oceanography. It presents oceanographic analyses based on real observational, satellite and reanalysis datasets.

The main objective is to transform oceanographic data into meaningful scientific information through data processing, visualization and interpretation.

Current analyses

01 · Sea Surface Temperature

Analysis of spatial and temporal variability of sea surface temperature in the Mozambique Channel.

Dataset: NOAA OISST v2.1

02 · Chlorophyll-a

Analysis of chlorophyll-a concentration and spatial patterns related to ocean productivity.

Dataset: NASA Ocean Color

Study Region

The initial analyses focus on the Mozambique Channel, approximately between 10°S–25°S and 35°E–48°E.
